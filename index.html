How to play

Drop a fresh 6×6 board. Groups of at least four identical candies connected horizontally or vertically disappear, and new candies fall into their places. Each winning candy cluster feeds its color collector. A full collector unlocks a six-drop boss battle; each normal matching cluster damages the boss. Defeat it to spin the demo reward wheel.

Four connected bombs clear neighboring tiles. Four or more connected lollipops award 12–22 free drops. Rainbow clusters open a jelly-picking bonus. The multiplier climbs on each cascade and carries across free drops. Bonus features wait their turn.

Original fan-inspired simulation. All balances, odds, thresholds, artwork and prizes are illustrative, not the commercial game's math. No deposits, cash redemption or real prizes.
